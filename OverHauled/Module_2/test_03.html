Squirrels

Squrriels are commonly thought of as tree-dwelling mammals, but the squirrel family extends for beyond that to include ground-dwelling rodents such as chipmunks and prairie dogs, and flying squirrels.

Two of the most common and best-known species are the:

  • Eastern gray squirrel
  • Red squirrel

For a good starting point on squirrel information, see the Wikipedia Squirrel page.
